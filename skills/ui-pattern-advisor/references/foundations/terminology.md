# Terminology And Confidence

Use four term statuses:

- **Standard**: broadly recognized in professional practice or platform-neutral literature.
- **Platform-specific**: an official or strongly established name within one platform ecosystem.
- **Common informal**: widely understandable shorthand without one normative definition.
- **Descriptive**: a useful composition label whose meaning comes from its words rather than an industry standard.

Do not infer standardization from confident wording, title case, or repetition across secondary material. When reverse-looking up a pattern, lead with the most precise supported label and expose alternatives when observable evidence is incomplete.

Confidence describes the classification and definition in this library, not the visual quality of the pattern:

- **High**: stable meaning and clear distinction from neighboring patterns.
- **Medium**: useful and coherent, but naming or boundaries vary.
- **Low**: provisional interpretation that requires more evidence before strong recommendations.
