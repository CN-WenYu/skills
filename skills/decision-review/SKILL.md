# Decision Review

Use this skill when two or more viable options have meaningful effects on cost, quality, risk, or reversibility. When the evidence is already decisive, state it and recommend the option directly instead of simulating a debate.

## Review

1. Restate the decision, candidate options, success objective, and hard constraints. Identify missing information that could change the outcome.
2. Choose two or three complementary perspectives that fit the decision, such as user value, engineering maintainability, and delivery or operational risk. Do not invent expert personas for ceremony.
3. Steelman every option: its strongest benefit, applicable conditions, largest risk, and hardest-to-answer objection.
4. Identify the real disagreement and the decisive variables without presenting preferences as facts.
5. If the decision depends on a testable hypothesis, design a low-cost, reversible, time-bounded minimal experiment. State the investment, observation metric, continuation condition, stop condition, and uncertainty it will resolve.

## Output

Provide the recommended option and rationale, conditions of use, largest risk, exit condition, the one missing fact that matters most, and the first action that can be taken now. Match confidence to the evidence.
